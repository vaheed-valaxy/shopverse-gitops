```text
1. ECR Registry Policy
   └── Must come from YOUR ECR
       └── No ECR credentials

2. Digest Policy
   └── Must use @sha256:<64 hex>
       └── No ECR credentials

3. Cosign Signature Policy
   └── Exact image must have trusted Cosign signature
       └── ECR credentials required

4. SBOM Policy
   └── Exact image must have trusted CycloneDX SBOM attestation
       └── ECR credentials required
```
