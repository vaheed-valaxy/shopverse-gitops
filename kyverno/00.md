```text
Kyverno
│
├── 01-ecr-only.yaml
│      └── Images must come from ECR
│
├── 02-image-digest.yaml
│      └── Main container must use @sha256:<64>
│
├── 03-image-signature.yaml
│      └── Image must be Cosign signed
│
└── 04-sbom.yaml
       └── Image must have SBOM attestation
```
**Policy 1 — ECR registry**  
```text
Is the image from our specific ECR?  (No ECR credentials needed)
```
   
**Policy 2 — Digest**  
```text
Does the image reference use @sha256:<64 hex characters>?  (No ECR credentials needed)
```
  
**Policy 3 — Cosign**  
```text
Can Kyverno verify the Cosign signature for this exact image?  (Private ECR access required)
```
  
**Policy 4 — SBOM**  
```text
Can Kyverno verify the signed SBOM attestation for this exact image?  (Private ECR access required)
```
